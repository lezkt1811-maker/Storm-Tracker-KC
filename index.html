✈️ Trail Check

Whether plane trails (contrails) should form right now, from forecast temperature and humidity where jets cruise. Jet exhaust contains water vapor; below about −40°F/−40°C it freezes into ice. In dry air the trail vanishes fast; in air saturated with respect to ice it lasts and spreads. Log what you actually see with the ➕ button (pick "Trails") and it's compared with this forecast.

How to Use This App

Plain-language version. Here's what each part does and a quick example to try.

🗺 MAP tab

Weather and an interactive map for Kansas City. The colored buttons above the map turn things on or off — try tapping "Aircraft" to see nearby planes appear. Scroll down and you'll find two more weather pictures (a radar loop and a country-wide Windy map) plus a rain forecast for the next few days. On the map itself, any bright blue/green/yellow/red is real rain — everything else (roads, parks, rivers) is muted on purpose so weather stands out.

✈️ Trail Check (Map tab)

Says whether plane trails should form and last right now, based on how cold and humid the air is up where jets fly. Try this: next time you see trails, tap ➕, pick "Trails," choose what they did, and save. It gets marked "matches the forecast" or "doesn't match." Over time your log shows how often what you see fits the weather.

🛰 Satellite buttons (Map tab)

"Clouds (infrared)," "Water vapor," and "Satellite color" add live GOES weather-satellite pictures to the map. Try this: turn on Water vapor and Aircraft together to see planes over moist vs. dry upper air.

✈️ Flight tracks

With Aircraft on, lines show where planes actually flew over a few minutes (magenta = above 25,000 ft). Tap a line for its flight number, altitude, and heading.

🎈 Atmosphere Above KC & 🌫 Aerosol Watch

The conditions at each height from ~5,000 to ~39,000 ft, and how hazy the air is compared with the past week.

📦 Event Packets (Vault tab)

When you see something odd, tap "Capture Event Packet Now." It saves a snapshot of every measurement plus satellite and radar pictures, with an ID and a fingerprint so it can't be quietly changed later. Open it any time to note which normal explanations fit.

⏱ TIMELINE tab

A list of things that happened, newest first — official storm warnings, plus anything you log yourself. It'll say "no events yet" until a warning is issued or you add one — that's normal, not broken.

⚠️ ANOMALIES tab

This is meant to tell you when something looks unusual, but it needs data collected over time first — it'll say "insufficient data" until then. Try this: go to the Vault tab and tap "Take Snapshot Now" a few times over a few days to start building it up.

🔬 LAB tab

A notebook for tracking a guess about weather — like "planes fly more before storms." Try tapping "+ New Hypothesis" to start one and see how it works.

🗃 VAULT tab

Settings and everything you've saved. This is where you check if things are working (System Status), turn on background data collection (Sentinel Mode), change your location, and download everything as a file (Export).

➕ Pink button (Map / Timeline)

Tap this to jot down something you personally noticed — a weird cloud, a sound, a plane. It's saved as your own note, kept separate from official weather data.
